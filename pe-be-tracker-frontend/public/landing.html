Email a question

Do I need an account to log workouts?

No. As a guest you log workouts by hand, saved in your browser. Sign in to log through chat, get recaps and keep your history on every device.

What does PersonalBestie cost?

Nothing. There is no subscription and no cap on routines.

How is PersonalBestie different from Hevy?

Hevy limits routines on its free plan. PersonalBestie adds chat logging and AI recaps. Compare PersonalBestie and Hevy

What can I type in a workout message?

Your own shorthand, like “bench 5x5 @ 50” or “ran 3 miles in 24 min”. You check the log before you finish.

Does it track running workouts?

Yes. Write the distance and time, and the run is saved beside your strength sessions.

Can I edit a workout after it is logged?

Yes. Open the workout to change any exercise, weight or rep, and add a note to any set.

Works with your own AI assistant

Connect any assistant that speaks MCP with a token from your profile. It can read your history, build routines and log sessions in the conversation.

You pick read-only or read and write, and revoke the token at any time.
