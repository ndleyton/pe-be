Questions before you start.

Do I need an account?

As a guest you can log workouts by hand and save them in your browser. Sign in for chat, recaps and access on other devices.

What does it cost?

PersonalBestie is free, with unlimited routines. Compare it with Hevy.

Does it track running?

Yes. Send “ran 3 miles in 24 min” to log distance and time alongside your strength workouts.

Can I edit a logged workout?

Open the workout to change an exercise, weight or rep count, or add a note to a set.

Works with your own AI assistant.

Connect an assistant that supports MCP with a token from your profile. Give it read-only access to your history or let it build routines and log sessions. You can revoke the token at any time.
